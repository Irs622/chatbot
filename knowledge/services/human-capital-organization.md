# INPARTNER Service: Human Capital & Organization

## Service Overview
Human Capital & Organization helps businesses build, develop, and optimize their people and organizational structures. INPARTNER provides executive search, organization development, talent and leadership advisory, and training and capacity building to help companies develop strong human capital foundations.

## Services Under Human Capital & Organization
1. **Executive Search / Head Hunting**
   - Professional executive search and head hunting services for C-suite and senior leadership roles.
   - Candidate identification, screening, assessment, and placement support.
   - Executive talent mapping and succession planning advisory.

   *Note: Executive Search / Head Hunting is a client-facing B2B service. This is distinct from INPARTNER's own internal recruitment activities.*

2. **Organization Development**
   - Organizational structure design, assessment, and improvement.
   - Governance framework development and organizational alignment.
   - Organizational effectiveness reviews and structural recommendations.

3. **Talent & Leadership Advisory**
   - Talent strategy development and talent management frameworks.
   - Leadership development advisory for executive and senior management teams.
   - Succession planning and high-potential employee development.

4. **Training & Capacity Building**
   - Structured training programs for corporate teams and leadership groups.
   - The Executive Business Program — a comprehensive curriculum for C-level executives, board members, general managers, and enterprise business owners.
   - Mentoring, coaching, and skill development programs.
   - Customized corporate in-house masterclasses tailored to specific organizational needs.

## Client Service Routing
| Client Problem | INPARTNER Solution |
|---|---|
| Need executive recruitment or headhunting | Executive Search / Head Hunting |
| Need to improve organizational structure | Organization Development |
| Need leadership development or succession planning | Talent & Leadership Advisory |
| Need training for teams or executives | Training & Capacity Building |
| Need coaching or mentoring for management | Training & Capacity Building (Executive Business Program) |

## Executive Business Program
The Executive Business Program is INPARTNER's flagship capacity building offering, designed for:
- C-level executives and Board Members
- General Managers and Enterprise Business Owners
- Senior Management Teams

Program formats include:
- Comprehensive executive curriculum
- Customized corporate in-house masterclasses
- Executive leadership coaching and mentorship (1-on-1 and small cohort)
- Execution toolkits and governance frameworks

## Who This Service Is For
- Companies requiring senior executive recruitment
- Organizations redesigning their organizational structure
- Leadership teams seeking development and capability enhancement
- Corporations investing in training and capacity building for their people
