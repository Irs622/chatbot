# INPARTNER Service: Investment & Project Advisory

## Service Overview
Investment & Project Advisory provides customized investment solutions for individuals, institutions, and corporate clients. INPARTNER's experienced professionals guide clients in making informed investment decisions, evaluating opportunities, and developing viable projects.

INPARTNER is not a bank, direct lender, broker, or investment fund manager. INPARTNER provides advisory in a consulting capacity to support client decision-making.

## Services Under Investment & Project Advisory
1. **Feasibility Studies (FS)**
   - Comprehensive commercial, financial, and operational feasibility assessments for projects and investments.
   - Market viability analysis, financial projections, and risk assessment.
   - Support for project approval, financing applications, and investor presentations.

2. **Investment Advisory**
   - Independent advisory supporting investment decisions for corporate and institutional clients.
   - Investment strategy development and portfolio guidance.
   - Assessment of investment opportunities across sectors and markets.

3. **Commercial & Financial Analysis**
   - Detailed commercial and financial analysis for business decisions, transactions, and projects.
   - Financial modeling, valuation, and scenario analysis.
   - Due diligence support for investments and acquisitions.

4. **Investment Opportunity Assessment**
   - Systematic evaluation of investment opportunities across sectors and geographies.
   - Opportunity screening, market sizing, and return analysis.
   - Assessment for corporate clients seeking to deploy capital strategically.

5. **Project Development**
   - End-to-end project development advisory from concept to execution.
   - Project structuring, stakeholder coordination, and implementation support.
   - Advisory for infrastructure, energy, real estate, and industrial projects.

## Client Service Routing
| Client Problem | INPARTNER Solution |
