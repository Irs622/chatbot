## Core Advisory Scope & Practice Areas

### 1. Bankable Feasibility Study (FS)
A Bankable Feasibility Study (FS) is INPARTNER's most rigorous investment advisory deliverable — independently produced to satisfy the scrutiny of lenders, investors, development finance institutions (DFIs), and joint venture partners.

INPARTNER's FS methodology covers:
- Market Viability Analysis: Demand forecasting, supply-side competitor mapping, addressable market sizing, and pricing corridor assessment.
- Technical & Operational Feasibility: Site selection logic, capacity configuration, capital expenditure (CAPEX) benchmarking, and operating cost structure (OPEX) with industry-standard norms.
- Multi-Scenario Financial Modeling: Integrated Base / Upside / Downside P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow models with NPV, IRR, DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio), Break-Even Point (BEP), and Payback Period calculated under each scenario.
- Risk Assessment & Mitigation Matrix: Identification of market, regulatory, execution, and financial risks with ranked likelihood/severity and recommended mitigation strategies.
- Regulatory & Licensing Navigation: Identification of licensing requirements (OSS-RBA, KBLI classifications, sectoral permits) and estimated timeline to project financial close.
- Investor/Lender-Ready Presentation Layer: Executive Summary aligned to institutional presentation standards for board approvals, DFI applications, and JV partner negotiations.

### 2. Commercial & Financial Analysis
- Integrated Financial Model Development: Dynamic multi-scenario Excel/Spreadsheet models covering revenue build-up, cost structure, EBITDA projections, working capital cycle, and debt service.
- Valuation Services (Management Consulting Basis): Indicative valuation ranges using Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation and Amortization (EBITDA) multiples, and asset-based approaches — for internal management decision-making, not regulated appraisal.
- Due Diligence Support: Commercial and financial due diligence for acquisition targets, JV partners, and strategic investments — validating revenue quality, customer concentration, margin sustainability, and working capital health.
- Sensitivity & Scenario Analysis: Stress-testing financial models against commodity price volatility, FX exposure, volume shortfalls, and interest rate shifts.

### 3. Investment Advisory & Opportunity Assessment
- Independent Investment Opportunity Evaluation: Systematic review of opportunities across sectors — identifying commercial logic, return profile, risk factors, and strategic alignment with client investment mandate.
- Investment Committee Memorandum Preparation: Preparing structured investment recommendation memoranda (Investment Memo / Information Memorandum — IM) for client boards and investment committees.
- Portfolio Screening & Sector Intelligence: Advising on capital deployment priorities based on sector growth outlook, regulatory tailwinds, and competitive positioning.
- Co-Investment & Strategic Partnership Facilitation: Identifying and qualifying potential co-investors, strategic partners, and anchor tenants for project development initiatives.

### 4. Project Development Advisory
- Project Conceptualization & Structuring: Translating business idea to investable project structure — defining scope, timeline, capital structure (equity/debt split), and governance.
- Information Memorandum (IM) Drafting: Preparing professional-grade IM documents for project financing presentations to banks, development finance institutions, and private investors.
- Stakeholder Coordination: Facilitating alignment across project sponsors, technical advisors, legal counsel, government agencies, and financial institutions.
- Project Finance Advisory: Advisory on project finance structures, including lender requirements, security packages, and off-take agreement frameworks — without acting as a lender or financial arranger.
- Milestone-Based Implementation Roadmap: Developing Gantt-structured project development roadmaps from permitting and engineering to commercial operations date (COD) and first revenue.
