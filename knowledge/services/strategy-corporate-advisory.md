# INPARTNER Service: Strategy & Corporate Advisory

## Service Overview
Strategy & Corporate Advisory is INPARTNER's premier management consulting pillar, dedicated to helping enterprise clients, holding conglomerates, and family-owned businesses define corporate direction, drive transformative growth, and navigate complex transactions including M&A, Pre-IPO readiness, and corporate restructuring.

We collaborate directly with C-suite executives, Board of Directors, enterprise founders, and investment committees to translate high-level vision into actionable, defensible corporate strategies that create measurable enterprise value.

## Core Advisory Scope & Practice Areas

### 1. Corporate Strategy & Transformation
- **Multi-Year Strategic Masterplan**: Formulating 3-to-5 year strategic roadmaps aligning corporate ambitions with industry shifts and macroeconomic conditions.
- **Business Model & Portfolio Optimization**: Evaluating core and non-core business units, product line profitability, and capital allocation across subsidiaries.
- **Operational Excellence & Margin Expansion**: Identifying operational bottlenecks, lean transformation, and cost-structure alignment (COGS/OPEX) to resolve profitability paradoxes.
- **Change Management & Execution Governance**: Designing management dashboards, executive KPIs, and cadence reviews to guarantee execution fidelity.

### 2. M&A Advisory (Mergers & Acquisitions)
- **Buy-Side & Sell-Side Strategic Advisory**: Identifying acquisition targets, evaluating corporate divestitures, and assessing strategic fits.
- **Commercial & Strategic Due Diligence**: Validating target market positioning, customer concentration, synergy potential, and realistic operational upside.
- **Deal Structuring & Valuation Support**: Assisting in transaction structuring, valuation range modeling (DCF, comparable multiples), and negotiating key commercial terms.
- **Post-Merger Integration (PMI) Planning**: Formulating 100-day integration plans covering organizational alignment, culture assimilation, system consolidation, and synergy realization.

### 3. Pre-IPO & Capital Market Advisory
- **Pre-IPO Diagnostic & Gap Analysis**: Assessing 12-to-24 month readiness across financial reporting, internal controls, tax compliance, and Good Corporate Governance (GCG).
- **Equity Story & Corporate Positioning**: Crafting compelling institutional investment equity stories highlighting defensive moats, scalable unit economics, and market upside.
- **Capital Restructuring & Holding Setup**: Restructuring shareholding hierarchies, cleaning up intercompany balances, and setting up clean holding vehicles.
- **Ecosystem Coordination**: Facilitating the selection and coordination of capital market supporting professionals (Lead Underwriters, Capital Market Legal Counsel, Public Accountants, Appraisers).
- **Secondary Capital Market Advisory**: Rights issues, private placements, and corporate debt restructuring strategies.

### 4. Corporate Restructuring & Family Business Succession
- **Turnaround & Debt Restructuring**: Designing operational turnaround roadmaps, renegotiating commercial payment obligations, and stabilizing working capital during liquidity distress.
- **Holding Company Architecture**: Structuring parent-subsidiary governance models to ring-fence operational liabilities and optimize centralized shared services.
- **Family Business Succession & Governance**: Formulating family constitutions, establishing clear board oversight vs management execution, and preparing next-generation leadership for seamless handover.
