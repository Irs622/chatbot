# INPARTNER Service: Strategy & Corporate Advisory

## Service Overview
Strategy & Corporate Advisory is INPARTNER's flagship consulting service, helping businesses develop clear corporate direction, execute transformations, and navigate complex corporate transactions including M&A, IPO, and restructuring.

INPARTNER works closely with C-suite executives, board members, and decision makers to formulate actionable strategies aligned with market realities and organizational capabilities.

## Services Under Strategy & Corporate Advisory
1. **Corporate Strategy**
   - Multi-year corporate strategy development and roadmapping.
   - Business model evaluation, portfolio optimization, and competitive positioning.
   - Alignment of corporate strategy with market opportunities and organizational capabilities.

2. **Business Transformation**
   - End-to-end business transformation advisory for organizations undergoing structural change.
   - Operational improvement, process re-engineering, and organizational redesign.
   - Change management support to ensure successful implementation.

3. **M&A Advisory (Mergers & Acquisitions)**
   - Advisory support for mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, and strategic transactions.
   - Target identification, commercial and financial due diligence, and deal structuring.
   - Negotiation support and transaction execution assistance.

4. **IPO / Capital Market Advisory**
   - Pre-IPO readiness assessment, preparation, and strategy development.
   - Capital market advisory for rights issues and other capital market activities.
   - Coordination with regulatory and market requirements for public offerings.

5. **Corporate Restructuring**
   - Operational and financial restructuring for organizations facing performance challenges.
   - Debt restructuring advisory and turnaround strategy development.
   - Organizational restructuring to improve efficiency and governance.

## Important Note — Regulated Activities
INPARTNER provides advisory services in the context of Business & Management Consulting. INPARTNER does not operate as a broker, asset manager, securities underwriter, or regulated financial service provider. All strategic and M&A advisory activities are conducted in a consulting capacity.

## Who This Service Is For
- CEOs, CFOs, and Board Members seeking strategic direction
- Companies planning major transactions (M&A, IPO, restructuring)
- Organizations undergoing business transformation
- Businesses requiring independent strategic advisory support
