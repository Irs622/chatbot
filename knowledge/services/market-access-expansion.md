# INPARTNER Service: Market Access & Business Expansion

## Service Overview
Market Access & Business Expansion is INPARTNER's commercial intelligence and partnership facilitation pillar. We help companies navigate Indonesia's complex market landscape, identify high-value strategic partners and distributors, and execute structured market entry and expansion strategies with precision.

We serve both Indonesian companies seeking domestic or international growth, and foreign companies pursuing market access and local partnerships in Indonesia. Our structured approach converts market ambiguity into actionable intelligence and qualified business introductions.

## Core Advisory Scope & Practice Areas

### 1. Market Research & Business Intelligence
- Market Sizing & Opportunity Assessment: Quantitative analysis of total addressable market (TAM), serviceable addressable market (SAM), and realistic obtainable market (SOM) across geographies and customer segments.
- Competitive Landscape Mapping: Identifying key players, their market positioning, pricing corridors, channel strategies, and strategic vulnerabilities.
- Consumer Behavior & Demand Analysis: Segmenting target customers by demographics, purchasing behavior, willingness-to-pay, and unmet needs.
- Regulatory & Compliance Intelligence: Mapping sectoral regulations, import/distribution licensing requirements, and regional market compliance conditions.
- Industry Trend & Growth Driver Analysis: Identifying structural growth tailwinds, technology disruption risks, and macro-regulatory trends affecting target markets.

### 2. Market Entry Strategy & Go-to-Market (GTM) Blueprint
- Go-to-Market (GTM) Framework Design: Defining channel architecture, pricing strategy, sales motion, and launch sequencing for new market entry.
- Market Entry Mode Selection: Evaluating direct sales, distributor model, agent network, joint venture, or e-commerce channel — with risk/return trade-offs for each mode.
- Regulatory Pathway Advisory: Identifying import/distribution licensing requirements (BPOM, SNI, sectoral Ministry permits) and advising on compliance sequencing.
- Geographic Prioritization: Evidence-based recommendation on which Indonesian regions (Java vs outer islands: Sumatra, Kalimantan, Sulawesi, Papua) to enter first based on demand concentration, logistics feasibility, and competitive intensity.
- Launch Roadmap & Commercial KPIs: Structured 90-day / 6-month / 12-month market entry execution calendar with milestone targets and commercial performance benchmarks.

### 3. Business Matching
- Structured B2B Commercial Introductions: Facilitating qualified introductions between companies, institutional clients, sector associations, and government-linked entities — not ad-hoc networking but curated, pre-qualified matching based on business fit, financial capacity, and strategic intent.
- Domestic Business Matching: Connecting companies with Indonesian corporate buyers, distributors, suppliers, joint venture candidates, and channel partners.
- International Business Matching: Facilitating introductions between Indonesian companies and overseas partners across INPARTNER's active network spanning South Korea, China, Japan, Singapore, Australia, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Switzerland, Belgium, Spain, Turkey, and South Africa.
- Facilitated Introduction Protocol: Pre-match due diligence, mutual background review, structured introduction meeting facilitation, and follow-up advisory support.

### 4. Distributor & Partner Identification and Vetting
- Long-List to Short-List Screening: Systematic market scan, initial outreach, and filtering of potential distributors and partners against client-defined criteria.
- Distributor Vetting Criteria: Assessment of logistics infrastructure and warehousing capacity, field sales force size and geographic reach, financial solvency and credit history, existing product portfolio and channel conflicts, and compliance and regulatory standing.
- Partner Negotiation Support: Advisory on commercial terms, exclusivity structures, margin frameworks, performance targets, and contractual protections.
- Vetted Distributor Shortlist Deliverable: Presenting a documented shortlist of 3–10 pre-screened, qualified partner/distributor candidates with profiles, assessment scores, and recommended prioritization.

### 5. Market Expansion Advisory
- Existing Market Penetration Deepening: Diagnosing root causes of market share stagnation and designing channel intensification or product repositioning strategies.
- Regional Expansion (Java to Outer Islands): Structuring logistics, distribution, and local commercial representation strategies for expansion beyond Java into high-growth outer-island markets.
- Cross-Sector Expansion: Advisory for companies entering adjacent industry sectors — leveraging existing brand equity and supply chain relationships to reduce new market entry risks.
- Export Market Development: Supporting Indonesian producers seeking to access export markets through market intelligence, buyer identification, and export compliance guidance.
