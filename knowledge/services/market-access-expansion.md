# INPARTNER Service: Market Access & Business Expansion

## Service Overview
Market Access & Business Expansion helps businesses navigate markets, identify opportunities, establish connections, and expand across markets. INPARTNER provides strategic intelligence and hands-on support for companies entering new markets or scaling existing market presence.

## Services Under Market Access & Business Expansion
1. **Market Research & Intelligence**
   - In-depth market research covering market size, growth trends, consumer behavior, and competitive landscape.
   - Industry intelligence reports and sector-specific market assessments.
   - Competitive benchmarking and market opportunity identification.

2. **Market Entry Strategy**
   - Comprehensive market entry strategy development for companies entering new geographic or sector markets.
   - Go-to-market (GTM) framework design, channel strategy, and launch roadmap.
   - Regulatory and compliance assessment for market entry.

3. **Business Matching**
   - Facilitation of strategic commercial introductions between businesses.
   - Connecting companies with relevant partners, clients, distributors, and institutional contacts.
   - Business matching across domestic and international markets.

4. **Partner / Distributor Identification**
   - Systematic identification and screening of potential partners and distributors.
   - Partner evaluation, shortlisting, and introduction facilitation.
   - Support for distribution network development and channel partner agreements.

5. **Market Expansion**
   - Strategic advisory for companies scaling existing market presence.
   - Geographic expansion planning across Indonesia and international markets.
   - Market penetration strategies for tier-1 and tier-2 markets.

## Client Service Routing
| Client Problem | INPARTNER Solution |
|---|---|
| Entering a new market | Market Entry Strategy |
| Need market research or intelligence | Market Research & Intelligence |
| Looking for partners or distributors | Partner/Distributor Identification |
| Want to scale or expand market presence | Market Expansion |
| Need business connections or introductions | Business Matching |

## Geographic Market Coverage
INPARTNER's market access capabilities span Indonesia and international markets including South Korea, China, Australia, Belgium, Malaysia, Singapore, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Switzerland, Spain, Turkey, and South Africa.

## Who This Service Is For
- Companies entering new geographic or sector markets
- Businesses seeking commercial partners or distribution networks
- Organizations requiring market intelligence and competitive analysis
- Corporations planning market expansion across Indonesia or internationally
