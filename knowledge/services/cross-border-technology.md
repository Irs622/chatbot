# INPARTNER Service: Cross-Border & Technology Advisory

## Service Overview
Cross-Border & Technology Advisory supports businesses in connecting international companies with local opportunities and developing cross-border business relationships. INPARTNER positions itself as a Cross-Border Bridge — facilitating market access, technology transfer, and strategic international partnerships.

## Services Under Cross-Border & Technology Advisory
1. **Cross-Border Partnership**
   - Facilitating strategic partnerships between companies across different countries.
   - Identification, evaluation, and introduction of international partnership opportunities.
   - Support for partnership structuring and agreement facilitation.

2. **Joint Venture (JV)**
   - Advisory for companies forming joint ventures across borders.
   - JV structuring, partner identification, commercial evaluation, and deal support.
   - Alignment of JV objectives with regulatory and operational requirements.

3. **Technology Transfer**
   - Advisory for technology transfer arrangements between international and local entities.
   - Technology licensing, commercialization, and knowledge exchange facilitation.
   - Assessment of technology transfer viability and regulatory compliance.

4. **Knowledge Transfer**
   - Facilitating the exchange of expertise, best practices, and organizational knowledge across borders.
   - Advisory for knowledge transfer programs between organizations in different markets.
   - Support for institutional capacity building through international knowledge exchange.

5. **International Business Development**
   - Strategic advisory for companies building or expanding international business relationships.
   - Identification of international opportunities, partner networks, and market entry pathways.
   - Support for cross-border business development initiatives and international expansion.

## Client Service Routing
| Client Problem | INPARTNER Solution |
|---|---|
| Need a cross-border partnership | Cross-Border Partnership |
| Considering a joint venture with an international partner | Joint Venture (JV) Advisory |
| Need technology or knowledge transfer | Technology Transfer / Knowledge Transfer |
| Building international business relationships | International Business Development |
| International company seeking local partner in Indonesia | Cross-Border Partnership / Business Matching |

## INPARTNER as a Cross-Border Bridge
INPARTNER's network spans multiple international markets including South Korea, China, Australia, Belgium, Malaysia, Singapore, UAE (including Abu Dhabi), Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Switzerland, Spain, Turkey, and South Africa. This positions INPARTNER to connect international companies with Indonesian market opportunities and vice versa.

## Technology as an Advisory Context
INPARTNER's technology-related advisory is conducted in the context of business consulting — specifically Technology Transfer, Knowledge Transfer, and supporting clients in technology-intensive sectors. INPARTNER does not operate as a software development company, IT service provider, or technology vendor.

## Who This Service Is For
- International companies seeking market access or local partners in Indonesia
- Indonesian companies seeking international expansion or partnerships
- Organizations requiring joint venture advisory across borders
- Businesses involved in or planning technology or knowledge transfer arrangements
