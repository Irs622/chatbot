# INPARTNER Service: Cross-Border & Technology Advisory

## Service Overview
Cross-Border & Technology Advisory is INPARTNER's international bridge-building pillar — connecting Indonesian companies to global partners, and channeling foreign capital and technology into Indonesia's high-growth sectors. We position ourselves as a structured, compliance-aware cross-border advisory firm that converts international ambition into actionable, bankable bilateral partnerships.

We serve foreign companies seeking Indonesian market entry, Indonesian companies pursuing international partnerships and expansion, and enterprises requiring Joint Venture (JV) structuring, Technology Transfer licensing, and cross-border business development.

## Core Advisory Scope & Practice Areas

### 1. Cross-Border Partnership Development
- International Partner Identification: Systematic identification of foreign companies, trade associations, and institutional entities whose strategic interests align with our client's business objectives.
- Partnership Feasibility Assessment: Evaluating the commercial logic, synergy potential, cultural fit, and regulatory compatibility of proposed bilateral partnerships.
- Bilateral Corridor Specialization: INPARTNER maintains active relationship networks across priority corridors — South Korea–Indonesia, Japan–Indonesia, Singapore–Indonesia, China–Indonesia, Europe (Belgium, Switzerland, Spain)–Indonesia, Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar)–Indonesia, and South Africa–Indonesia.
- Partnership Structuring Advisory: Advising on partnership formats — including distribution agreements, reseller arrangements, referral frameworks, equity co-investment, and strategic MOU structures — aligned with Indonesia's Foreign Investment (PMA) regulatory framework.
- Negotiation Facilitation: Neutral advisory role in cross-border commercial negotiations, ensuring mutual commercial clarity and protecting client interests at critical deal junctures.

### 2. Joint Venture (JV) Advisory
- JV Partner Identification & Screening: Systematic identification and assessment of potential JV partners — evaluating financial capacity, operational track record, governance standards, and strategic alignment.
- JV Commercial Structure Design: Advising on ownership percentage frameworks, capital contribution structures, profit-sharing mechanisms, governance rights, and exit provisions — balancing commercial objectives with Indonesia's KBLI (Standard Industrial Classification) foreign ownership restrictions.
- PMA & OSS-RBA Regulatory Navigation: Guiding foreign investors through Indonesia's Online Single Submission Risk-Based Approach (OSS-RBA) licensing system, PMA company establishment, and KBLI sector restrictions for joint venture entities.
- JV Term Sheet & Commercial Framework Advisory: Drafting and reviewing key commercial terms in JV Heads of Agreement (HoA), Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), and Shareholders' Agreements — in coordination with client's legal counsel.
- JV Governance & Operational Setup: Designing board governance structures, management authority matrices, and operational protocols for newly formed joint ventures.
- Post-JV Monitoring & Advisory: Advisory support during the initial operational phase of the JV, covering performance milestone tracking, governance compliance, and escalation protocols.

### 3. Technology Transfer Advisory
- Technology Transfer Viability Assessment: Evaluating the commercial, technical, and regulatory feasibility of transferring proprietary technology from a foreign entity to an Indonesian recipient.
- Technology Licensing Framework: Advising on licensing structures — including exclusive, non-exclusive, and sub-licensing arrangements — and royalty rate benchmarking.
- BPOM, SNI & Sectoral Regulatory Compliance: Identifying applicable Indonesian regulatory requirements for technology-intensive products (pharmaceuticals, medical devices, processed food, industrial equipment) including BPOM registration, SNI certification, and Ministry technical approvals.
- Intellectual Property (IP) Protection Advisory: Coordinating with client's IP counsel to ensure adequate protection of transferred technology under Indonesian IP law (Patents, Trade Secrets, Industrial Design).
- Recipient Entity Assessment: Evaluating the operational, financial, and organizational capacity of Indonesian recipient entities to successfully absorb and commercialize transferred technology.
- Government & Development Agency Facilitation: Supporting engagement with government agencies (BRIN, BPOM, relevant Ministries) facilitating technology transfer programs aligned with Indonesia's industrial policy priorities.

### 4. Knowledge Transfer Programs
- Institutional Knowledge Transfer: Designing structured programs for transferring organizational methodology, operational best practices, and institutional knowledge from an international organization to its Indonesian counterpart.
- Capacity Building for Technology Recipients: Designing training curriculum, operational manuals, and certification pathways to ensure sustainable operational capability after technology or knowledge transfer.
- Cross-Border Management Exchange: Advisory and facilitation for management secondment, executive exchange, and mentoring programs between international and Indonesian entities.
- Academic & Research Institution Linkages: Facilitating knowledge transfer partnerships between international research institutions, universities, and Indonesian counterparts.

### 5. International Business Development
- Market Entry Advisory for Foreign Companies: Comprehensive advisory for foreign companies entering Indonesia — covering market intelligence, regulatory landscape, local partner identification, and go-to-market strategy.
- Indonesian Company Internationalization: Strategic advisory for Indonesian enterprises pursuing regional or global expansion — including market selection, regulatory compliance, and partner identification in target countries.
- International Investor Introduction: Facilitating introductions between Indonesian project sponsors and foreign institutional investors, strategic corporates, and development finance institutions (DFIs) active in the region.
- Trade & Investment Mission Support: Organizing and facilitating commercial delegation meetings, B2B forums, and bilateral trade sessions aligned to client business development objectives.
