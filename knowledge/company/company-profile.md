# INPARTNER Company Profile

## About INPARTNER (PT Inpartner Optima Integra)
INPARTNER, legally PT Inpartner Optima Integra, is a premier Business & Management Consulting firm headquartered in Jakarta, Indonesia. Publicly known as Inpartner Consulting, we operate with the tagline "Unleash The Power Of Your Business" and the strategic vision of "Bridging Markets, Investment & Business Opportunities."

INPARTNER is not a bank, direct lender, securities brokerage, investment fund, IT company, or regulated financial services provider. INPARTNER is a professional management consulting firm providing advisory services across corporate strategy, investment, market access, cross-border partnerships, and human capital.

## Company History & Evolution
INPARTNER's origin dates to 2009, when the founding practice began supporting MSME (Micro, Small & Medium Enterprise) clients in East Java across market access, financing facilitation, technology advisory, and capacity building.

In 2019, the practice formally transformed into a Business & Management Consulting firm, expanding its client focus to serve mid-sized and large corporations, state-owned enterprises (SOEs), foreign investors, and government-linked entities across Indonesia and international markets.

These are not contradictory dates: 2009 represents the business origin, and 2019 marks the transformation to the current corporate consulting model with the 5-pillar service framework currently offered.

3. Identify — Systematic identification of opportunities, strategic options, qualified partners, and investable assets.

5. Execute & Grow — Implementation support, market entry execution, and sustained business growth advisory.

## Commercial Engagement Protocol
When a corporate client engages INPARTNER, the collaboration follows a professional process designed to ensure confidentiality, mutual alignment, and measurable outcomes:

Step 1 — Exploratory Diagnostic Consultation: An initial session where INPARTNER's senior advisors understand the client's business objectives, immediate challenges, and strategic priorities. This session is without obligation and conducted to assess whether INPARTNER's capabilities are the right fit.

Step 2 — Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA): Before any proprietary business information is shared, INPARTNER and the client execute a bilateral Mutual NDA — ensuring full confidentiality of client strategies, financials, and commercially sensitive information.

Step 3 — Tailored Terms of Reference (ToR) / Statement of Work (SoW): INPARTNER prepares a customized scope of work documenting specific advisory deliverables, methodology, timeline, and commercial terms — uniquely tailored to the client's situation.

Step 4 — 5-Stage Execution (Understand → Analyze → Identify → Connect → Execute & Grow): Structured execution of the agreed scope with milestone-based delivery and regular progress reviews with client stakeholders.

Step 5 — Milestone Delivery & Impact Review: Deliverables (reports, models, introductions, roadmaps) are presented at agreed milestones. Post-engagement impact review evaluates outcomes against the original strategic objectives.
