## What is a Bankable Feasibility Study and does INPARTNER produce them?
Yes. A Bankable Feasibility Study (FS) is a comprehensive, independently produced assessment evaluating the commercial, technical, financial, and regulatory viability of a project or investment — structured to satisfy the documentation requirements of lenders, investors, and joint venture partners. INPARTNER's FS deliverable includes integrated multi-scenario financial models (.xlsx) with NPV, IRR, DSCR, Break-Even Point (BEP), and Payback Period under Base, Upside, and Downside scenarios.
