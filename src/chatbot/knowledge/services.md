# INPARTNER Official Advisory Services

INPARTNER provides 5 integrated service pillars structured across corporate strategy, investment, market entry, cross-border technology, and organizational capability.

---

## 1. Strategy & Corporate Advisory
Equipping Indonesian and international enterprise leadership with actionable strategies to scale, reorganize, and unlock shareholder value.

### Key Advisory Practices:
- **Corporate Strategy & Transformation:** Long-term business modeling, market repositioning, digital and operational transformation roadmaps.
- **M&A Advisory (Buy-Side & Sell-Side):** Strategic target identification, commercial & operational due diligence, deal negotiation structuring, and Post-Merger Integration (PMI).
- **Pre-IPO & Capital Market Advisory:** 12–24 month capital market readiness diagnostics, holding company restructuring, equity story preparation, and underwriter/legal counsel coordination.
- **Corporate Restructuring & Turnaround:** Capital reorganization, debt and balance sheet restructuring, operational cost realignment.
- **Family Business Succession & Governance:** Family constitution formulation, board governance architecture, and generational wealth transition planning.

---

## 2. Investment & Project Advisory
De-risking capital investments and structuring bankable infrastructure and commercial projects across Indonesia.

### Key Advisory Practices:
- **Bankable Feasibility Studies (FS):** Independently validated feasibility reports designed to meet the rigorous credit documentation standards of commercial lenders, state banks, and private equity funds.
- **Multi-Scenario Financial Modeling:** Integrated 3-statement financial models with dynamic sensitivity analysis (NPV, IRR, DSCR, Payback, BEP) across Base, Bull, and Bear scenarios.
- **Investment Opportunity Assessment:** Location scouting, risk-reward profiling, Capex/Opex benchmark studies, and sovereign incentive analysis.
- **Information Memorandum (IM) Preparation:** Institutional-grade investor documentation for debt, mezzanine, or equity fundraising.
- **Project Development Advisory:** Project structuring, public-private partnership (KPBU/PPP) alignment, and contractor/operator selection.

---

## 3. Market Access & Business Expansion
Enabling international companies (notably South Korean investors) and regional businesses to enter, capture, and dominate the Indonesian market.

### Key Advisory Practices:
- **Market Research & Business Intelligence:** Granular TAM/SAM/SOM market sizing, regulatory entry barriers, consumer demand patterns, and competitive benchmarking.
- **Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy:** Distribution architecture, pricing strategies, and regional commercial rollout roadmaps.
- **Structured Business Matching:** Bilateral B2B matchmaking with vetted corporate buyers, industrial off-takers, and commercial partners.
- **Distributor Identification & Vetting:** Multicriteria screening of national and regional distributors evaluating logistics capability, financial solvency, field network, and licensing standing.
- **Regional & International Market Expansion:** Assisting Indonesian corporations in outbound expansion across ASEAN and East Asia.

---

## 4. Cross-Border & Technology Advisory
Specialized advisory bridging technology transfer, joint venture partnerships, and bilateral industrial collaboration between South Korea and Indonesia.

### Key Advisory Practices:
- **Joint Venture (JV) Structuring:** Shareholder agreement advisory, voting rights, deadlock resolution, management appointment mechanisms, and minority protections.
- **Foreign Direct Investment (PT PMA & OSS-RBA):** Legal and operational navigation of Indonesia's Positive Investment List (Perpres 10/2021 as amended by Perpres 49/2021), minimum capital requirements (IDR 10B+ investment per KBLI), and OSS Risk-Based Licensing.
- **Cross-Border Technology Transfer:** Licensing agreements, intellectual property protection, localization of Korean manufacturing and cleantech technologies.
- **Knowledge Transfer & Industrial Capacity Building:** Human capital training programs to ensure smooth operational absorption of advanced foreign technical systems.
- **Bilateral Bilateral Partnership Management:** Bridging cultural, linguistic, and regulatory nuances between Korean executive boards and Indonesian stakeholders.

---

## 5. Human Capital & Organization
Building organizational resilience, board capability, and executive talent to support strategic execution.

### Key Advisory Practices:
- **Retained C-Level Executive Search & Head Hunting:** Confidential recruitment of Managing Directors, CEOs, CFOs, COOs, and specialized country managers for international entrants and domestic corporations.
- **Organization Design & RACI Architecture:** Redesign of corporate hierarchies, reporting matrices, key performance indicators (KPIs), and decision-making rights.
- **Leadership & Talent Advisory:** Executive competency assessment, leadership development blueprints, and executive succession planning.
- **Corporate Training & Capacity Building:** Tailored corporate training, including the flagship Executive Business Program.
