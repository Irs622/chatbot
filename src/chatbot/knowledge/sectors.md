## Indonesia-Korea Bilateral Investment & Regulatory Framework

### IK-CEPA (Indonesia-Korea Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement)
- Tariff eliminations on over 92% of Indonesian tariff lines and 95% of Korean tariff lines.
- Preferential investment rules protecting Korean investments against arbitrary expropriation, ensuring capital repatriation, and facilitating executive visas.

### Regulatory Guidelines for Foreign Direct Investment (PT PMA):
1. **Minimum Capital Requirement:**
   - Under BKPM/Ministry of Investment regulations, a foreign-owned entity (PT PMA) requires a minimum total investment of IDR 10,000,000,000 (excluding land and buildings) per 5-digit KBLI business classification code.
   - Minimum paid-up capital is IDR 2,500,000,000 (or IDR 10,000,000,000 depending on recent Perka BKPM updates).
2. **Positive Investment List (Perpres 10/2021 & Perpres 49/2021):**
   - Replaced the restrictive Negative Investment List. Most business sectors are open up to 100% foreign ownership, except those reserved for domestic MSMEs or partnerships.
3. **OSS Risk-Based Approach (OSS-RBA):**
   - Business licensing categorized into Low, Medium-Low, Medium-High, and High Risk.
   - Low Risk requires only an NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha). Higher risks require Standard Certificates and specialized verification licenses (Izin Operasional/Komersial).
4. **Tax Incentives & Special Economic Zones (KEK):**
   - Corporate Income Tax Holiday (up to 100% reduction for 5 to 20 years for pioneer industries).
   - Tax Allowance (30% reduction in net taxable income spread over 6 years).
   - Super Tax Deduction for vocational training and R&D activities (up to 200% and 300% respectively).
