## 2. Korea-Indonesia Bilateral Investment & Cross-Border Advisory

### How does INPARTNER assist South Korean companies entering Indonesia?
INPARTNER provides end-to-end advisory for Korean corporates:
1. **Market Intelligence & Sizing:** Sector feasibility studies and regulatory risk mapping.
2. **PT PMA Setup & OSS-RBA Licensing:** Guidance on minimum investment capital (IDR 10B per KBLI), foreign equity caps under Perpres 10/2021, and environmental/building permits (AMDAL, PBG).
3. **Local Joint Venture (JV) Structuring:** Finding and vetting credible Indonesian conglomerates or business partners, drafting shareholder agreements, and protecting foreign IP.
4. **Government Liaison & Tax Incentives:** Assisting in applying for Tax Holidays, Tax Allowances, and KEK (Special Economic Zone) benefits.
5. **Executive Search:** Recruiting bilingual (Korean-Indonesian-English) leadership and local C-level executives.

### What is the minimum capital requirement for establishing a PT PMA in Indonesia?
Under current Indonesian investment regulations (BKPM):
- Total investment plan must be greater than **IDR 10,000,000,000** (ten billion Indonesian Rupiah) excluding land and buildings per 5-digit KBLI business category.
- Issued and paid-up capital must be at least **IDR 2,500,000,000** (or IDR 10,000,000,000 for certain licensed operations).
- INPARTNER helps foreign investors optimize KBLI classifications to ensure compliance while minimizing redundant capital commitments.

### Does INPARTNER support technology transfer and licensing?
Yes. Under our Cross-Border & Technology Advisory pillar, INPARTNER assists in structuring technology transfer agreements, protecting intellectual property rights (IPR) in Indonesia, and setting up technical training frameworks for local personnel.

## 3. Advisory Practices & Deliverables

### What is a Bankable Feasibility Study (FS)?
A Bankable Feasibility Study is an institutional-grade, independently prepared technical and commercial report designed to satisfy the rigorous lending and investment criteria of state banks (Himbara), commercial banks, international finance institutions (IFC, ADB), and private equity funds. INPARTNER's FS includes dynamic multi-scenario financial models (NPV, IRR, DSCR, Payback Period) and detailed regulatory compliance audits.

### Does INPARTNER handle M&A (Mergers & Acquisitions)?
Yes. INPARTNER provides Buy-Side and Sell-Side M&A advisory, commercial due diligence, target valuation modeling, deal negotiation support, and post-merger integration (PMI) planning.

### Does INPARTNER assist companies planning an Initial Public Offering (IPO)?
Yes. Under Pre-IPO & Capital Market Advisory, INPARTNER performs 12–24 month diagnostic gap analyses, corporate holding restructuring, equity story preparation, and acts as the company's independent advisor liaising with underwriters, legal auditors, and accountants.

### Can INPARTNER help our company identify vetted distributors in Indonesia?
Yes. Our Distributor Identification & Vetting service screens national and regional distribution partners against strict operational criteria: field sales strength, logistics and warehousing capacity, creditworthiness, and regulatory permits.
